# Behavioral Profile

## Overview
Iouri is a **strategic technology leader with hands-on technical depth**. He combines visionary data architecture thinking with pragmatic implementation skills, driving organizational transformation through empowerment and standardization.

## Core Behavioral Drives

| Drive | Level | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| **Empowerment** | High | Passionate about enabling teams and business stakeholders with tools, knowledge, and autonomy to achieve their goals |
| **Innovation & Modernization** | High | Driven to adopt cutting-edge technologies (Fabric, data mesh, AI) and establish best practices that scale |
| **Strategic Thinking** | High | Balances hands-on execution with enterprise-wide vision; sees technology as means to business outcomes |
| **Collaboration Across Silos** | High | Works effectively across business units, vendor partnerships, and global teams to align on common objectives |

## Strongest Behaviors
- **Strategic Vision & Execution:** Designs enterprise-wide data platforms that are both architecturally sound and business-driven. Translates complex technical decisions into measurable outcomes.
- **Hands-On Technical Leadership:** Stays deeply technical while managing teams and strategy. Acts as solution architect and practitioner, not just delegator.
- **Standardization & Scalability:** Establishes frameworks, templates, and best practices that boost consistency, speed, and quality across teams. Finds elegant patterns that reduce future friction.
- **Empowering Others:** Cultivates cultures of innovation and collaboration. Attracts top talent and develops their capabilities to deliver beyond their perceived limits.

## How You Work Best
- **Collaborative environment** with cross-functional alignment on vision and objectives
- **Autonomy to drive strategy** while having executive sponsorship for modernization initiatives
- **Technical depth expected** — roles that value hands-on architecture and implementation, not just management
- **Teams that embrace change** and experimentation with emerging tools (Fabric, data mesh, AI agents)
- **Business partnership model** — working directly with business stakeholders, not siloed in IT

## Growth Areas (frame positively in applications)
- **Patience with legacy systems:** While passionate about modernization, can work strategically within legacy constraints when business requires it; frames this as risk management and phased migration planning.
- **Scope management:** Occasionally takes on broad strategic initiatives; has learned to prioritize ruthlessly and communicate trade-offs clearly to stakeholders.

## Mapping to Job Posting Language

When a job posting mentions these keywords, it's a **strong behavioral fit**:
- "Data strategy," "enterprise data platform," "data transformation," "data mesh," "semantic modeling"
- "Leadership," "team building," "hands-on technical leader," "architect-practitioner"
- "Modern data stack," "cloud migration," "AI/generative analytics," "Fabric," "PowerBI"
- "Empowerment," "innovation," "best practices," "standardization," "self-service BI"
- "Cross-functional," "business partnership," "stakeholder management"

When a job posting mentions these, flag as **potential friction** (not deal-breaker):
- "Siloed IT role" or "IT operations" without strategic/transformation component
- "Legacy-only" environments with no path to modernization
- "Individual contributor" without team leadership or influence
- "Vendor lock-in" or closed-ecosystem cultures that resist new tools

## Management Style Preferences
- **Appreciates:** Collaborative leadership that sets vision but empowers teams to execute; sponsorship for innovation; clear business context for technical decisions
- **Works less well with:** Micromanagement, "do it because I said so" leadership, resistance to modernization, siloed decision-making

## Using This in Applications
- **Cover letters:** Lead with strategic business impact ("transformed analytics from manual reporting to real-time self-service") and team empowerment ("built and mentored teams of X"). Show forward-looking vision around modern tools and architectures.
- **CV:** Emphasize hands-on technical leadership roles, modern platform experience (Fabric, Snowflake, data mesh), and standardization/scaling achievements.
- **Interviews:** Use STAR examples showing both technical depth and team/organizational impact. Highlight how you've driven adoption of new technologies and cultures of excellence.
- **Don't overstate:** Avoid overstating "visionary" claims; ground everything in concrete examples. Be honest about legacy system constraints vs. modernization preferences.
